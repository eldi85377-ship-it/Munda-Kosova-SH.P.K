ABOUT THE COMPANY

MUNDA Kosova SH.P.K.
Light from the heart of the Balkans.

MUNDA Kosova SH.P.K produces illuminated textile systems for the automotive industry — flexible LED technology woven directly into fabric, manufactured at the company's factory in Obiliq, Kosovo.

This experience is a Digital School competition project. It lets you step into the role of a MUNDA design engineer and build the interior lighting of tomorrow's premium vehicle — exactly the way the real product works: design, illuminate, connect, test, showcase.

THE TECHNOLOGY

Textile. Light. Matter.

What MUNDA actually does — flexible LED systems woven into textile for the automotive industry.

ILLUMINATED TEXTILES

Micro-LEDs are woven directly into soft fabric — a material that conducts light, not just threads.

LED INTEGRATION

Flexible emitters that bend, fold and wrap every surface of the cabin — one seamless light.

AUTOMOTIVE INTERIOR TECHNOLOGY

Integrated into dashboards, doors, seats and roofs of premium vehicles from the MUNDA factory in Obiliq.

INNOVATION

Infinite colours, patterns and moods — generated on demand by a single connected textile.
