THE COMPANY

MUNDA Kosova SH.P.K.
Light from the heart of the Balkans.

MUNDA Kosova SH.P.K produces illuminated textile systems for the automotive industry — flexible LED technology woven directly into fabric, manufactured at the company's factory in Obiliq, Kosovo.

The Kosovo subsidiary of MUNDA Textile Lichtsysteme GmbH — the joint venture of AUNDE and MENTOR — and the first supplier approved by the Volkswagen Group in Kosovo, delivering components for brands like Audi.

Joint venture MUNDA Textile Lichtsysteme GmbH — founded 2019 by AUNDE & MENTOR, Erkrath, Germany.
Audi A3 Facelift 2024 — the first series-production car with MUNDA textile light in the door trims.
Obiliq plant MUNDA Kosova SH.P.K. since March 2023 · plant inaugurated April 2025.

THE TECHNOLOGY

Textile. Light. Matter.

What MUNDA actually does — flexible LED systems woven into textile for the automotive industry.

ILLUMINATED TEXTILES

Micro-LEDs are woven directly into soft fabric — a material that conducts light, not just threads.

LED INTEGRATION

Flexible emitters that bend, fold and wrap every surface of the cabin — one seamless light.

TEXTILE LIGHT SYSTEMS

Large illuminated surfaces, free shapes and dynamic light — drapable and safe in any zone of the cabin.

INNOVATION

From yarn to light.

How MUNDA brings a new technology from the laboratory to the road.

2019 The joint venture

AUNDE & MENTOR found MUNDA Textile Lichtsysteme GmbH in Erkrath — technical textiles meet LED technology.

2023 Kosovo steps in

MUNDA Kosova SH.P.K. is registered in Obiliq with €500,000 capital — production starts close to home.

2024 World premiere

The Audi A3 Facelift is the first series vehicle with MUNDA textile lighting in its door trims.

2025 Inauguration

The Obiliq plant opens officially — Kosovo's first Volkswagen Group-approved supplier delivers components.
